Everything you need to use Tomato Crop Doctor effectively

🚀 Getting Started

ℹ What is Tomato Crop Doctor?

A machine learning web app that identifies diseases on tomato plants from a photograph of a leaf, fruit, stem, flower or roots. It first detects which part is in the photo, then uses a Keras model trained for that part, and finally suggests remedies.

🌱 Detected Parts

Leaf, fruit, stem, flower and roots, each with its own disease model (a part is available once its model is installed).

🍃 Leaf Conditions

  • Bacterial Spot
  • Early Blight
  • Late Blight
  • Leaf Mold
  • Septoria Leaf Spot
  • Spider Mites
  • Target Spot
  • Yellow Leaf Curl Virus
  • Mosaic Virus
  • Healthy

📱 System Requirements

  • Browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions)
  • Image Format: JPG, PNG supported
  • File Size: Maximum 16MB per image
  • Resolution: Minimum 256×256 pixels recommended

🧭 How to Use

  1. Open the Home page and upload a clear photo of one tomato plant part. Keep Auto-detect selected, or pick the part yourself.
  2. Press Analyze Image and wait for the result.
  3. Answer the additional questions on the result page.
  4. Press Get Remedies to see treatment and prevention steps.
  5. Find past scans any time on the History page.

📊 Understanding Results

The predicted disease is the class the model finds most likely. The confidence level shows how sure it is. Below about 70%, retake the photo or consult an agriculture expert before treating.

💡 Tips & Best Practices

  • Photograph in daylight, avoiding harsh shadows.
  • Fill the frame with a single part (one leaf, one fruit, a stem section) on a plain background.
  • Choose a part that shows the symptoms clearly.
  • Scan several leaves if the plant looks badly affected.

🛠 Troubleshooting

  • Upload fails: check the file is JPG or PNG and under 16MB.
  • Low confidence: use a sharper, closer photo.
  • Wrong result: make sure the image is a tomato plant part, since the models only know tomato.
